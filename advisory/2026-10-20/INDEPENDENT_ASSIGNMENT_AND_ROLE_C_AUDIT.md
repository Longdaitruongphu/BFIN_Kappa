## Decision and consistency finding

The original-mandate package is computationally reproducible from saved source files. A network-disabled rebuild completed on 20 October, and its 28 recorded numerical/integrity checks passed. A separate read-only audit hashed all 22 saved market/factor download files and independently recomputed **1,320** monthly security returns and **528** monthly FF3 values from the raw bytes. Maximum absolute errors versus the processed files were `6.39e-16` for returns and `2.78e-17` for factors; details are in `outputs/role_c_audit/source_chain_receipt.json`. A second independent standard-library script revalued the five bond quotes, initial budget, payment coverage, goal arithmetic, risk-preference comparison, and the 36-month cost-inclusive Sharpe/performance figures; its receipt is `outputs/role_c_audit/core_equations_receipt.json`. These establish internal provenance and arithmetic for the saved downloads, not that Yahoo prices were never revised, that the chosen universe was unbiased, or that the forecast is true. No new live vendor download was used for this audit.

The original recommendation remains **Beta $2,214,672; Delta $3,429,808.70; USMV $6,346,350.71; assumed entry costs $9,168.59**. These sum to $12 million. Beta matures into the $2.4 million 2024 obligation. Delta's final coupon plus principal covers the $3.6 million 2027 obligation; its four earlier coupons accumulate to $486,956.52 at a conservative zero reinvestment rate. The fixed payments consume $6 million in nominal cash in total, equivalent to $1.2 million or 10% of initial capital per year if merely spread across 2023–27; this annualization is an explanatory average, **not** five actual yearly payments. At 2.5% annual inflation, $8 million of December 2022 purchasing power requires $9,747,223.18 nominal in 2030. The USMV sleeve must compound at 4.8365% for eight years under this cash convention. The 7.1425% forecast for USMV is an *arithmetic* annual expected return, not a promised CAGR.

Historical monthly risk-free rates are the **RF column in the contemporaneous-month French FF3 file** for both 2015–22 estimation and 2023–25 evaluation. The 4% rate in the policy is a **prospective annual planning input** for CAPM expected-return comparisons, not a substitute for historical RF or a guaranteed cash yield. The 2.5% inflation assumption converts the 2030 real goal into nominal dollars and deflates scenarios; it does not discount fixed nominal 2024/2027 payments. The base deliberately assumes zero reinvestment on surplus bond coupons. `analysis/config.json`, `outputs/tables/goal_sensitivity.csv`, and `outputs/tables/risk_preference.csv` preserve these distinct uses.

The retrieval settings are explicit in `analysis/retrieve_data.py` and compatible with the [yfinance Ticker.history interface](https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.Ticker.history.html). The [Kenneth French factor definitions](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library/f-f_factors.html) identify Mkt-RF, SMB, HML and RF. The raw ZIP stores factor returns in percent units; the code divides by 100 once, then subtracts RF from the asset's monthly decimal return for CAPM/FF3. It never adds Yahoo dividend events on top of adjusted-close returns.

The 15% concern is an escalation trigger rather than a maximum possible loss. The combined -30% equity / +100bp curve stress marks a 17.53% initial-wealth loss even though the payment cash flows remain covered. The forecast preference parameter `A=4` is a board judgement, not a value identified from the vague 15% statement. Within the two predeclared ETF choices, `A<2.5803` favors VTI, while an equity risk premium above 6.9759% favors VTI at `A=4`. Any proposal to change that assumption needs a recorded governance reason. The frozen 2023–25 comparison is unfavorable to USMV: after assumed entry costs, it returned 37.33% cumulative (Sharpe 0.66) versus VTI's 82.56% (Sharpe 1.30). The original 2022 selection cannot be rewritten using that later outcome.

## Role C workbook: what passes and what fails

The independent comparison is recorded in `outputs/role_c_audit/role_c_independent_audit.json`; its read-only script is `analysis/audit_role_c.py`. The workbook should remain a source document, not be silently merged into the frozen decision.

1. `Returns_data!A1:K97` has 96 monthly returns for **HD, JNJ, JPM, MSFT, NEE, PG, UNH, USMV, VTI, XOM**. All 768 values for its eight tickers shared with the project match the saved project returns within `1.11e-6` absolute (rounding scale). Its HD and UNH values cannot be traced to this project's raw files. Matching processed values is useful arithmetic evidence, not proof of Role C's claimed independent download.
2. `Stock Returns!A1:K96` uses a **different** 95-month universe: **AAPL, BAC, JNJ, JPM, KO, MSFT, PFE, PG, QQQ, SPY**. This conflicts with Role C's own `Returns_data` list and the project's frozen ten candidates. The five overlapping series agree with the project to `5.23e-6` absolute, consistent with rounding. Ninety-five observations still exceed the assignment's minimum, but the different universe must not be used to explain the original USMV growth decision without an explicit new rationale and data record.
3. `Fama-French Factors!A1:E97` exactly matches the project's 96 processed FF3 months, including RF. Role C's hard-coded `Multifactor Regression!A1:F11` coefficients and R-squared values independently reproduce from its own 95-month rounded return table to at most `1.45e-5`. Thus the displayed regression numbers are arithmetically plausible. They are for the alternative universe, omit the chosen USMV growth sleeve, and contain no uncertainty intervals or persistent-alpha test; they cannot satisfy the original decision's factor explanation by themselves.
4. `C3_return check!A27:H36` is **not a trustworthy pass record**. MSFT row 27 uses manually rounded prices 153.27 and 149.21: `D27/C27-1 = -0.02648920206`, while linked `Returns_data!E64` is `-0.026541298`. The difference is `0.00005209594` in return units, but `G27` is hard-coded zero and `H27` says `PASSED`. Rows 28–36 have blank input prices, cached `#DIV/0!` calculations, and hard-coded `PASSED`. Replace these checks with actual unrounded raw prices, live difference formulas, numerical tolerances and a reviewer/date before treating them as verification.
5. `C4_source record!A2:N16` names `raw/yahoo_monthly_raw.csv` and SHA-256 `52a38d9cf96a8233d716e65d5e78449a42748fed5e397c22dd450b964c46a4f0`, but that raw file was not supplied with the workbook or found in this workspace. The two summary rows have blank SHA cells, and their named raw files were likewise absent from the supplied material. The claim is **unverifiable from the attachment**, not proof that the data were invented. The project's separate Yahoo/FF3 raw files, manifest and retrieval code do not retroactively substantiate Role C's own source-record hash.
6. `C1&2_eligibility!M2:N11` contains no evidence-file path or independent checker for its ten securities; current Yahoo profile links are not dated case-date evidence. `Optimization` is empty. These cannot serve as the assignment's eligibility, independent-check or sample-versus-single-index evidence. The project has separate outputs for those requirements.

The project-side eligibility record also has a limitation: direct SEC downloads failed with HTTP 403; `eligibility_checks.json` is a dated summary of official pages inspected via web access, not locally saved SEC filing bytes. A stronger contemporaneous first-party USMV existence record is the issuer's [13 June 2022 SEC-filed prospectus supplement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/930667/000119312522172131/d334785d497.htm), which lists the USMV fund among the affected iShares funds and points to its then-current prospectus. Its existence supports eligibility, not an unbiased historical selection or future performance. This new reference is an audit addendum and does not alter the frozen model.
