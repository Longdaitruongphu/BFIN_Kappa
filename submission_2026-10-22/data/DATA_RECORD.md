# Dated source and verification record

**Case information cutoff:** 31 December 2022. **Raw download:** 15 October 2026 UTC (exact timestamps per file in `raw/retrieval_manifest.jsonl`). **Independent saved-source check:** 21 October 2026. The downloaded vendor histories are retrospective; the download date is not the information date used by the decision model.

## Raw inputs

- Yahoo Finance via `yfinance` 1.7.0, `Ticker.history`, for AAPL, MSFT, JPM, JNJ, PG, XOM, CAT, NEE, USMV, VTI. Exact ticker URLs, timestamps, returned row counts, hashes and package versions are in `raw/retrieval_manifest.jsonl`. Request: 2014-12-01 inclusive to 2023-01-01 exclusive, daily; `auto_adjust=False`, `back_adjust=False`, `actions=True`, `repair=False`, `keepna=True`. Saved original untransformed CSV responses are in `raw/train/`. The final trading day is 30 December 2022. One extra December 2014 observation supplies the January 2015 return baseline.
- Kenneth French Data Library, [Fama/French 3 Factors monthly ZIP](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/ftp/F-F_Research_Data_Factors_CSV.zip). Its original downloaded ZIP bytes and SHA-256 are preserved. The monthly 2015-01 to 2022-12 rows are extracted offline. The source expresses Mkt-RF, SMB, HML and RF in **percent**; the model converts to decimal by dividing by 100. French's full vendor archive contains later dates, but they are never fed to the initial decision.
- Case bond quote prices and fixed payment dates come from pages 1–2 of the supplied BFIN assignment, not Yahoo. These fictional bonds have no historical traded quote series. The script computes only their contractual coverage and stated initial cost.
- Case-date equity eligibility was checked against pre-2023 SEC filings and ETF documents. URLs and filing dates are in `supporting/eligibility_checks.json`. Direct SEC file downloads returned HTTP 403, logged in `raw/supporting/source_register.json`, so those are **inspection notes, not saved raw SEC filings**. Four successfully downloaded supporting documentation pages are retained in `raw/supporting/` with hashes. The source chain for market prices and FF3 is separate. Eligibility in 2022 does not prove unbiased ex ante candidate selection.

## Construction and checks

Use last available trading-day `Adj Close` in each calendar month and `P_t/P_(t-1)-1`. Yahoo adjusted close is used as the vendor's corporate-action-adjusted return proxy; `Close`, dividend and split fields remain in the raw CSV to inspect differences. No missing values are filled, no return is fabricated and all ten assets share 96 months. Historical excess returns subtract the **observed monthly French RF**, while prospective CAPM uses the explicit **4% annual planning RF scenario**. The 4% is not an eight-year cash offer. Candidate annual volatility is monthly sample standard deviation times sqrt(12). The 8-year CPI goal uses 2.5%; a 3.0% education-cost stress is evaluated separately. Coupon cash earns 0% in the base calculation.

31 December 2022 was not a trading day. Yahoo's 30 December close is the final pre-decision market observation and a valuation input, **not an executable 31 December ETF quote**. The 10bp equity entry friction is a planning assumption, not a verified broker fill.

The package's `run_initial.py` rechecks eleven saved training-source SHA-256 hashes and four supporting-file hashes, checks the request settings, case-date cutoff, common-month coverage, missing prices, French units and full USD 12m allocation. An independent project-level check recomputed every one of the 960 asset-month training returns and 384 monthly training factor values from saved raw files; across its broader audit, the maximum absolute return and factor discrepancies were below 7e-16 and 3e-17. Only the pre-2023 training inputs are delivered here.

**Limitations:** Yahoo and French can revise historical prices/factors after the case date. No point-in-time vendor tape was available. SEC eligibility notes are source references, not archived filing bytes. CAPM return forecasts, fees, inflation and risk aversion are transparent assumptions, not observed 2022 market guarantees. There was no missing Yahoo/French training download after successful retries; SEC HTTP 403 failures were logged separately and were not silently replaced by invented records.
